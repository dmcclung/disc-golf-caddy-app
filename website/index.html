1. Closed plant foot

Your front foot lands pointing past the target, so your hips can't open and the disc pulls left. Plant with your toe at the target and let the hips lead.

WHAT YOUR CADDY LOOKS AT

Six parts of every throw

Footwork

X-step timing, stance, and where your plant foot lands.

Reach back

Disc path, depth, shoulder turn, and staying on plane.

Power pocket

Pulling the disc close through your chest with the elbow leading.

Weight shift

Moving from back to front, hips leading, bracing on the plant leg.

Release

Nose angle, hyzer or anhyzer, and a clean snap out of the grip.

Follow through

Rotating through the brace and finishing balanced.

Works for backhand, forehand, approach shots and putts. Lefties welcome.

FAQ

Questions

How should I film my throw?

From the side, about 30 feet away, with your whole body in frame from your run-up to your follow through. Prop your phone on your bag or a tripod. Good light helps a lot.

How does the AI analyze my video?

The app pulls about ten key frames from your clip, clustered around your release if you mark it. Those frames go to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, along with your skill level and recent sessions. It returns scores, faults and drills in a few seconds to a minute.

Is my video uploaded?

Only the still frames used for your analysis are uploaded and stored with your report. The full video stays on your phone. You can delete any throw, or your whole account, from the app.

Is it as good as an in-person coach?

Nothing beats a great coach standing next to you. DG Caddy is for all the field sessions in between: fast, honest feedback every time you throw, so you're not grooving bad habits.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. Cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, or from Settings in the app.
